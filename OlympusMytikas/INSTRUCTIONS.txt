===============================================================================
WOW FOREVER - INSTRUCTIONS FOR OUR ADDONS
===============================================================================

Install the OlympusMytikas folder in _forever_/Interface/AddOns. Keep your
WTF folder to preserve saved settings. The merged addon replaces the old
OlympusRecruit and KillOnSight folders. Part 2 covers the separate MineWatch
addon for players who also have it installed.

===============================================================================
PART 1 - OLYMPUS MYTIKAS (version 1.1.4)
===============================================================================

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. OPENING THE WINDOW
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Type /mytikas (or /omg), or left-click the OlympusMytikas minimap button.
- The window shows your installed version and a selectable Member Home
  address. Use the Recruit and Kill on Sight tabs to switch tools.
- Minimap button: left click = window, right click = post one random ad,
  drag = move it anywhere in or around the minimap.
- /om minimap hides or shows the button.
- The addon has its own logo in the WoW AddOns list. A full restart is needed
  before a new texture shows up.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. RECRUIT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Post an ad:
- /om with no argument posts ONE random ad to /1 General and /2 Trade.
- In the Recruit tab: choose an ad with < / >, then click "Post this ad" or
  "Post a random ad".
- /om pick <number> selects an ad from the keyboard.
- Add ads in the ad box with "Add ad"; remove the selected one with "Del".
- There is NO auto-post. Forever only accepts a chat send from a real click or
  key press, so every post must be something you actually press.

Guild invite line:
- Right-click a player and use "Invite to <guild>". Forever forbids addons from
  sending guild invites. The addon opens a box with /ginvite First Surname
  already selected. Copy it, close the box, paste into chat and press Enter
  yourself. Names are "First Surname", not "Name-Realm".
- /om invite <name> opens the same box from the keyboard.

Key bindings (Key Bindings > Olympus Mytikas):
- "Post a recruitment ad"
- "Open the Olympus Recruit window"

Other useful commands:
- /om help  - list Recruit commands.
- /om why   - show menu-hook and blocked-action status.
- /om version - show the build; /om version off|on toggles newer-version notices.
- /mytikas hidden (or /mytikas chat) - replay the last 50 hidden Olympus-family
  chat lines. Replies to commands you type are never hidden.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. KILL ON SIGHT - THE BOARD
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- /kos opens the board.
- Right-click a name in the board or death feed to start a bounty, or use
  /kos bounty <name> / /kos fund <name>, or click the Fund button.
- A bounty is LIVE at once and is a flat 1 gold. No bank visit, no deposit
  check, no pending state.
- The requester owes that 1g until a guild master or Valar clears it. The
  addon never moves gold. A person pays at the guild bank; the addon records.

Tabs in the KoS window:
- Board       - the enemy list and the death feed.
- Hunter Board - this month's top hunter by completed funded bounties.
- Ledger      - WHO / GOLD / FOR / WHEN / STATUS, with Prev/Next paging.

The board has no mouse-wheel scrolling. Use Prev/Next. Rows stay still during
guild sync; a genuinely new kill moves to the top, newest first.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. MONEY AND DEBTS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- 70/30: the guild bank keeps 30%; the hunter's 70% joins ONE prize pool for
  the month. The hunter with the most completed bounties wins the pool.
  A person pays the winner by hand; /kos boardpaid records that hand-over.
- /kos owed shows who owes what. The Officers button posts the same owed line
  into chat for the bank officers.
- GM or Valar: /kos paidup <name> clears a requester's debt. The "Mark paid"
  button does the same and has a hover explanation. Use it only after the 1g
  has actually reached the guild bank.
- /kos paid explains the prize pool.
- The addon reads balances for /kos status and /kos bank, but does not check a
  deposit before starting a bounty and never moves gold.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. KILLS, LEVEL AND LAYER
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- /kos kills (or /kos kills mine) shows recorded deaths.
- Bounties show the killer's level and layer when readable: board row, detail
  panel, death-feed tooltip, Announce and /kos where. Level comes from visible
  units, tooltips, the roster or Olympus evidence; layer comes from LayerSwap.
  Unreadable values stay blank - never guessed.
- A layer only starts on a NEW kill and lasts one hour. Old kills do not start
  with a layer. After an hour it retires on every client.
- Your own layer is used only at the moment you actually die, because the
  killer was on your layer then. Later sightings do not borrow it.
- /kos where [name] prints the last known location and the level/layer reads.
  Map pins last 30 minutes after the last sighting; the board keeps the spot.
- /kos swap [name] (or the "swap to layer" button) opens a chat box with a
  plain whisper to the victim who set the bounty, asking for a layer invite.
  Press Enter to send it. Hidden LayerSwap notes let a host auto-invite.
  Old or expired kills refuse.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. CHAT, WARNINGS AND MAP
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Kill/death notices are NOT printed in normal chat. /kos chat replays them;
  /kos chat clear wipes the buffer. Command replies still print normally.
- A local "BOUNTY IN RANGE" warning and a nameplate icon appear per client for
  live funded bounties you can see. The addon does not set Blizzard's real
  raid skull and does not send RAID_WARNING chat.
- /kos badge (or /kos marks) explains which visible nameplates get a badge.
- /kos pins reports the map-pin state.
- Prefix-matched automatic Olympus-family local lines are hidden to reduce
  spam. Use /mytikas hidden to see them.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. TURNING KoS OFF
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- The KoS ON/OFF button in the Olympus Mytikas window toggles the kill board.
  Off hides the KoS tab and stops notices, sync, badges and map pins.
  Recruit keeps working. The setting is saved account-wide.
- /kos off and /kos on do the same from the keyboard.
- Run with /kos off when you only want recruiting.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. DELETES AND SYNC
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Only the guild master or Valar can delete an entry. A plain member cannot
  delete even with a force flag, and a forged delete from a member is ignored.
- A manager's delete is final on old and new clients. To bring a name back,
  add it again by hand with /kos add or right-click and start a new bounty.
- There is no "master mode" and no /kos master command.
- Guild sync keeps board rows still while you read them, and brings new kills
  in at the top.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. QUICK COMMAND LIST - OLYMPUS MYTIKAS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  /mytikas              open the shared window
  /om                   post one random ad to /1 and /2
  /om pick <n>          select ad number n
  /om invite <name>     open the /ginvite copy box
  /om version           show build / toggle newer-version notices
  /mytikas hidden       replay hidden Olympus-family chat lines
  /kos                  open the Kill on Sight board
  /kos bounty <name>    start a live 1g bounty
  /kos fund <name>      same as bounty
  /kos owed             list who owes gold
  /kos officers         post the owed line to chat
  /kos paidup <name>    GM/Valar only: clear a requester's debt
  /kos boardpaid        record the monthly prize hand-over
  /kos chat             replay held kill/death notices
  /kos chat clear       clear that buffer
  /kos kills            show recorded deaths
  /kos where [name]     show last location, level and layer
  /kos swap <name>      whisper the victim to ask for a layer invite
  /kos announce         announce the selected killer to guild chat
  /kos badge            explain visible nameplate badges
  /kos pins             show map-pin state

  /kos help             full in-game command list

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. FIRST TIME
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The first time Kill on Sight loads, a rules window opens. Tick "I have read
this" and click continue. You can replay it any time with /kos notice.
Full change history: Interface\AddOns\OlympusMytikas\CHANGELOG.txt


===============================================================================
PART 2 - MINEWATCH
===============================================================================

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHAT IT DOES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

MineWatch warns you when ANOTHER PLAYER comes close while you are out mining.
It shows a pulsing banner with their class-coloured name, and can play a sound.
It is read-only: it never casts, interacts with a node, or sends game input.

It only arms when YOU cast a mining spell. Standing at a vein, looting, or
other game objects does not arm it. Once armed, the window stays open about
2 minutes; a mining skill-up or ore loot extends it. If nobody is near, it is
silent.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE FIRST THINGS TO DO
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Type /mw once. The options window opens.
- /mw test shows a fake warning so you can see and hear it.
- /mw list is the diagnostic. It shows the mining window, the last cast, every
  visible nameplate and whether it would alert, the soft target, and a verdict.
- Friendly players are invisible on this client unless friendly nameplates are
  on. MineWatch turns them on automatically by default. If you turned them
  off, /mw friends puts them back. /mw list will say if none are on screen.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. THE WARNING
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- A pulsing banner appears with the names of nearby players.
- Sound is ON by default. /mw sound toggles it.
- The alarm is a shipped sound file (siren by default). /mw alarm cycles
  through siren, beeps, horn and client kit sounds, plays a preview, and saves
  your pick. /mw alarm <number> chooses one directly.
- Each new player makes it beep again. With the default linger 10, the banner
  hides 10 seconds after the last change. /mw linger 0 keeps it up until
  they leave.
- /mw unlock (or /mw move) lets you drag the banner; then /mw lock fixes it
  in place. It is locked by default so it cannot eat clicks.
- /mw chat toggles the addon's own chat messages.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. RANGE AND WHO COUNTS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Default radius is 28 yards. /mw radius 10, 28 or 40, or /mw 10 etc.
- By default ALL players count, friendly or enemy. /mw enemies switches to
  enemies only. /mw enemies again puts it back.
- /mw on and /mw off turn the whole addon on or off.
- Default: mining-only alert. /mw gather turns that OFF so it alerts anywhere;
  use /mw gather again to restore mining-only. The default is deliberately
  mining-only so it does not go off while you are questing or in town.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. DIAGNOSTICS IF IT DOES NOT ALARM
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  /mw list    - every plate the addon can see, with tags like player, friendly,
                npc, dropped:far, dropped:not-gathering, and the final verdict.
  /mw cast    - the last cast id/name, what the client calls it, and whether it
                would arm the window. Use this DURING a real mining cast.
  /mw bags    - dumps bag/equipped item reads and the mining pick, if found.
  /mw art     - texture check window. A blank square means that file did not load.
  /mw skin    - switches between the drawn gold frame and the client's dialog
                frame if the art does not look right.
  /mw reset   - restore all defaults.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. QUICK COMMAND LIST - MINEWATCH
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  /mw                 open the options window
  /mw on | off        enable or disable the addon
  /mw test            show the warning banner and play the alarm
  /mw sound           toggle the sound
  /mw alarm [n]       cycle or choose the alarm, and preview it
  /mw chat            toggle the addon's chat messages
  /mw gather          toggle mining-only arming
  /mw enemies         toggle enemies-only filtering
  /mw friends         turn friendly nameplates back on
  /mw linger [s]      banner linger; 0 = until they leave
  /mw radius [yd]     10, 28 or 40 yards
  /mw list            full diagnostic of plates, window and verdict
  /mw cast            last cast and whether it arms the window
  /mw bags            bag / equipped item reads for the mining pick
  /mw lock            lock the banner position
  /mw unlock          drag the banner, then lock it
  /mw art             texture check
  /mw skin            switch between drawn art and client dialog frame
  /mw reset           restore defaults
